1-Click generation

Automation that keeps up with your sprints.

A typical implementation ships to UAT every two weeks, and testers get about one day with it. With script-based tools, updating automation after each sprint takes weeks, so coverage falls behind. Boson removes that gap.

STEP 1

Connect

Point Boson at your Salesforce org or ServiceNow instance. It runs on your own infrastructure.

STEP 2

Boson reads your build

It interprets Lightning Web Components, OmniScripts, Flows, triggers, Apex and Visualforce. No code required from you.

STEP 3

Generate

One click produces complete end-to-end user journeys, ready to run in your pipeline.

STEP 4

Update

Next sprint, Boson finds every change and its impact. One click updates the whole suite. Patented self-healing keeps runs reliable.
